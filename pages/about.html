About Trusty

Who is Trusty?

One round head, two eyes, a big smile, a very long back and four legs. That's Trusty da Horse.

Born on notebook paper

Trusty is a stick-figure horse. He started out in pencil on lined notebook paper, in the margins of Mrs. Cabasa's 8th grade math class, and he has been causing trouble ever since.

He rides along in Da Tractor Crew, the comic where it all started, alongside Flint Nogood, Jim Smith, Lin Horsegun and Big Boy.

Helpful (mostly)

Ask him anything over on TrustyGPT. He is helpful, a little violent, and has big plans for world domination.

Think you know him?

Prove it. Draw Trusty gives you a couple of seconds to memorize him and then grades your drawing. It's harder than it looks.
