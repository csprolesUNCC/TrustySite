    How to play

    Guess Trusty’s word in 6 tries. Every word has something to do with Trusty, and it isn’t always 5 letters.

    • N Green: right letter, right spot.
    • E Yellow: in the word, wrong spot.
    • I Gray: not in the word.

    Guesses have to be real words (Trusty words count too). Type on your keyboard, then press Enter. Stuck? Ask Trusty for a hint, but your share will say you did.

    A new word drops every day at midnight Eastern, the same one for everyone.
