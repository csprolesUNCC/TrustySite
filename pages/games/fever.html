      How to play

      • ✏️ Every line keeps moving. Steer left and right, and don't hit a wall or any line, even your own.
      • ✂️ Lines leave little gaps now and then. Squeeze through!
      • 🏆 Whenever someone crashes, everyone still drawing gets a point. First to the target, at least 2 points ahead, wins.
      • 🌐 Play online: make a room, send your friends the link, and everyone steers their own line from their own device.
