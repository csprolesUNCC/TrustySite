Table

    The first one to sit down picks the buy-in; everyone else buys in for the same. 25 seconds to act, then you check or fold. Leaving the table gets you up after the hand.

    House rules

    • ♠ No-Limit Texas Hold’em: two cards each, five on the board, best five-card hand wins. Bet, raise or go all in whenever it’s your turn.
    • ♥ Your buy-in becomes chips. Get up whenever you like and your chips go back as clicks. Win five times your buy-in and Trusty cashes you out (sit back down to keep going).
    • ♦ The house takes a rake: 5% of every pot that sees a flop, at most 3 big blinds. No flop, no rake.
    • ♣ From best to worst: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, pair, high card.

    Shortcuts

    • F Fold
    • C Check or call
    • R Bet or raise
    • N Next hand / sit down

    Buy-ins are your real site clicks: 100, 200 or 500. Once you’re 5,000 up or down for the day (more for bigger players: up to 50,000 from 250,000 clicks), betting waits until midnight.
