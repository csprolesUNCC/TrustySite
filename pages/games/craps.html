    House rules

    • ⚀ On the come-out roll, the Pass Line wins on 7 or 11 and loses on 2, 3 or 12. Any other number becomes the point.
    • ⚁ Then keep rolling: the point again wins the Pass Line, a 7 first loses it. Don’t Pass is the other way round (and a 12 on the come-out is a push).
    • ⚂ Once there’s a point, put Odds behind your Pass Line, up to twice it. They pay true odds: 2 to 1 on 4 and 10, 3 to 2 on 5 and 9, 6 to 5 on 6 and 8.
    • ⚃ Field, Any Seven and Any Craps are one-roll bets: they win or lose on the next roll.
